Frequently Asked Questions ♡

How CareText works, how your loved ones stay in control, and what to expect from each plan.

Can my loved ones respond to the texts I send via CareText?

Yes. When CareTouch sends an SMS, your loved one can reply to the CareText number. Their reply will appear in your secure CareText dashboard, and you can receive a notification. The first release will not automatically forward replies to your personal phone or send AI-generated replies.

Are loved ones charged for replying?

CareText does not charge your loved one a subscription fee for replying. Their mobile carrier may apply standard messaging or data charges depending on their plan.

Am I charged for texts I send through my own phone?

CareText does not charge an SMS-delivery fee when you copy a MyTouch suggestion and send it through your own messaging app. Your carrier’s normal messaging and data rates may apply.

How does my loved one opt in?

You share a personal invitation link directly. Your loved one reviews the CareText disclosure, actively agrees to receive messages, and verifies their phone number. Adding their number to your account does not grant consent. They can decline or opt out.

Will my loved one know the message was generated with AI?

Yes. In MyTouch, you copy the suggestion and send it personally; CareText does not append a disclosure to your personal text, so you decide how to explain the assistance. In CareTouch, recipients are informed during opt-in that AI helps create messages, and the CareText-delivered messages identify the sender and service. In standard CareTouch, you approve messages before delivery. With Trusted Autopilot, eligible subscribers may opt into AI-generated automatic delivery without reviewing each message.

What is the difference between MyTouch and CareTouch?

MyTouch helps you remember occasions and create text suggestions to copy into your own texting app. CareTouch sends your approved messages from a CareText number on a schedule after your recipient consents. Free includes MyTouch only; CareTouch requires a paid plan.

What if my loved one declines or opts out after I subscribe?

CareText stops sending to that person. You can invite a different loved one to use your available recipient slot, subject to consent and plan limits. If you cannot find an eligible replacement, you can request a review for a refund or credit through support; refunds are not automatic or guaranteed. For first purchases, the proposed refund request window is 7 days for monthly subscriptions or 14 days for annual subscriptions. Requests are reviewed under the published refund policy and applicable law; refunds are not automatic. You can also cancel renewal or choose the Free plan for a future billing period. We won't automatically downgrade an active paid subscription just because someone opts out.

How often can I replace a loved one on Essential or Plus?

Essential allows one active loved one; Plus allows up to five. Both plans permit one voluntary completed recipient replacement per account in any rolling 30-day period, not one per recipient slot. Declined invitations, documented consent withdrawals, and incorrect numbers may qualify for verified exceptions. Unaccepted invitations do not count as replacements. A replacement must opt in before CareTouch can send messages.

What is LearnMe™?

LearnMe™ is optional. When enabled, it uses your choices, edits, regeneration feedback, and optional example messages to build a personal writing-style profile. When off, CareText provides suggestions without learning a persistent style. You can turn it off or reset the style in Settings.

How many AI-generated texts are included for free?

Five new AI-generated texts per month, including regenerated versions. Editing or copying an existing suggestion does not use another generation. The Free plan does not send SMS through CareText.

What are the daily texting limits?

Daily CareTouch scheduling requires Plus. Plus includes up to 100 CareText SMS per billing month shared among up to five loved ones. Essential includes up to 15 CareText SMS per month for one loved one, with no daily sending. Special-occasion texts count toward the allowance.

Can I cancel a text after I send it?

You can edit or cancel a message while it is still pending. Once an SMS is submitted to the mobile network, CareText generally cannot recall it. A short cancellation delay for immediate CareTouch sends is planned.

What happens if someone texts STOP?

CareText will stop sending messages as required by the recipient’s opt-out request. The recipient can also decline the invitation before any recurring CareTouch texts begin.

Will CareText sell my information?

No. CareText’s product policy is not to sell subscribers’ or recipients’ personal information. The final privacy policy will explain service providers, security measures, data retention, and deletion rights before launch.

Do I have to approve every CareTouch message?

Initially, yes: messages must be reviewed and approved before scheduled delivery. After 90 days of eligible use, Trusted Autopilot may be available as an explicit opt-in, with safeguards and recipient transparency.

What is the refund window?

For an initial purchase, you may request a refund within 7 days for a monthly subscription or 14 days for an annual subscription. A request does not guarantee approval; the published refund policy and applicable law govern. Service failures and other documented exceptions may be reviewed separately. Canceling renewal does not automatically refund the current period.

How do renewals work?

Paid subscriptions will be processed through Polar and renew automatically according to the billing schedule shown at checkout. Customers cannot change the charge date through the self-service portal. Annual plans are offered at a discount; cancellation terms will be shown before purchase.

Do standard texting rates apply?

Standard message and data rates may apply through your mobile carrier for texts you send or receive, depending on your mobile plan. CareText subscription allowances are separate from carrier charges.
