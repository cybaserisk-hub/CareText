Privacy Policy

CareText is the customer-facing brand of CyBase Risk Management, LLC. This policy covers information handled through caretext.app. CareText is currently in private testing. Public registration, paid checkout, live SMS delivery, and Trusted Autopilot are not enabled.

Information we collect and use

For private account services, we store account email and authentication records, recipient names and phone numbers entered by a subscriber, relationship details, personalization preferences, scheduling choices, and optional LearnMe™ examples. We use these records to authenticate users, save their setup, secure accounts, and provide support. Authentication emails are delivered through Resend. Supabase stores account and application data. Hosting and security services process technical information needed to provide and protect the website.

The planned SMS service will also process recipient verification and consent evidence, approved messages, delivery information, replies, opt-outs, and suppression records. When billing is enabled, billing providers will process payments; CareText will use subscription and transaction references rather than store full card or bank credentials. These services are not currently active.

LearnMe™ controls

LearnMe™ is optional and off by default. When enabled, it stores writing examples to support personalization. To withdraw this choice, turn LearnMe™ off and select Save setup. A successful save deletes saved LearnMe™ examples and writing-style history from the active application database. Recipient details and ordinary setup preferences are separate and are not deleted by this control. Live AI generation and ongoing learning from replies or edits are not enabled.

This deletion does not mean that every provider backup is erased immediately. Backup copies and provider logs follow the provider's supported lifecycle; CareText has not yet established a verified automated backup-deletion schedule.

SMS consent, replies, and opt-outs

A subscriber entering another person's number does not grant SMS permission. Before recurring CareText messages can start, the recipient must affirmatively agree to the disclosed program and complete the required phone verification. Consent applies to the identified subscriber and recipient relationship. SMS consent is not a condition of purchase.

For the planned SMS program, reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Replies will be processed to handle recipient requests and, where enabled and disclosed, route replies to the requesting subscriber. They are not a confidential support channel. Consent and suppression evidence may need to remain after an opt-out so the preference can be honored. STOP/HELP processing and reply routing must be verified before live delivery starts. Messages a user copies into a personal texting app are outside CareText's delivery system.

Mobile information and service providers

Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This restriction also applies to lead generators.

Necessary information may be processed by service providers supporting authentication, hosting, storage, security, email, and, when enabled, AI, payments, and SMS delivery. Such processing is for operating CareText, not for those providers' independent marketing or promotional use of SMS consent data. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect against fraud and abuse.

Storage, retention, and deletion

The following are CareText's proposed operational retention targets, subject to applicable law and verification of service-provider capabilities. They are not universal Texas statutory retention requirements. Except for active-database LearnMe™ deletion described above, these schedules have not yet been fully implemented or verified. They must not be read as guaranteed deletion deadlines during private testing.

Data categoryProposed retention policy
Active accounts and recipient profilesRetain while needed for service delivery; delete or anonymize within 30 days of a verified eligible deletion request, unless retention is legally required.
Message drafts, generated texts, and repliesRetain while the relevant account is active; delete within 30 days after eligible account deletion, subject to legal exceptions.
LearnMe™ examples and learned-style historyStop using the information for personalization immediately when disabled; promptly delete active stored examples and learned history. In the current app, select Save setup to apply disabling and deletion to the server.
SMS consent recordsRetain for five years after withdrawal or the last relevant communication, subject to applicable legal requirements. The retention trigger must be defined in the implemented records procedure.
SMS opt-out suppression recordsRetain only the minimum information necessary, for as long as needed to prevent future unwanted messages.
Billing and transaction recordsRetain necessary accounting records for seven years, subject to applicable legal requirements. Do not store full payment card or banking credentials.
Security and operational logsRetain for 90 days unless needed for an investigation, dispute, or legal hold. Provider-controlled logs may have different supported retention periods.
BackupsTarget deletion or expiration within 90 days, subject to actual provider capabilities and legal preservation requirements. A restoration procedure must reapply deletions before restored data is used; this safeguard is not yet implemented.

Account and recipient data currently remain until authorized deletion is carried out. Account deletion may require separate handling of consent, suppression, accounting, and audit evidence. CareText does not promise immediate removal from all provider backups. Provider retention and deletion settings must be verified before the operational targets become published service commitments.

Privacy rights and requests

Customers and recipients may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to identity verification and applicable legal exceptions. Send privacy requests to privacy@caretext.app. Inbound delivery has been tested successfully. This address forwards to the mailbox designated by the operator as monitored.

Our proposed procedure is to acknowledge requests within five business days and target a substantive response within 30 calendar days, or sooner when legally required. Identity and authority must be verified before protected information is disclosed or deleted. Applicable extensions, appeals, and additional rights will be honored. We will explain records retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or SMS consent purposes. Request tracking, response coverage, and escalation procedures must be finalized before launch.

Identify whether you are a subscriber or recipient and the account email or phone number involved. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary sensitive documents. A request does not authorize disclosure of another person's records. For the SMS program when enabled, recipients may withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP; minimum suppression information will be retained to honor opt-outs. For support, contact support@caretext.app; inbound delivery to this address has also been tested successfully. notifications@caretext.app is used for outbound transactional notifications and is not a verified inbound privacy or support channel.

Security

CareText uses authenticated access, ownership-based database controls, and encrypted network connections. No service can guarantee absolute security. Contact us if you believe your account or information has been misused.
