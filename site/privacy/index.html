Privacy Policy

Draft — effective date to be confirmed

CareText helps people stay connected through reminders, AI-assisted writing, and consent-based SMS delivery.

Information we collect

We may collect account details, loved-one profiles and phone numbers, relationship preferences, message drafts, approvals, consent records, replies, subscription status, and technical logs. Optional LearnMe™ may store writing samples, selections, edits and feedback to personalize suggestions.

How we use information

We use data to provide accounts, generate suggestions, deliver approved messages, verify SMS consent, handle replies and opt-outs, manage subscriptions, provide support and prevent abuse. Necessary information may be processed by contracted hosting, AI, email, billing and SMS providers.

LearnMe™ controls

LearnMe™ is optional. When turned off, CareText does not create or update a persistent writing-style profile. Turning it off removes saved LearnMe™ examples and history as implemented, subject to legally required records. Users can request account deletion.

SMS consent

Automated CareText SMS requires affirmative recipient consent and phone verification. Recipients may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Consent records may be retained to demonstrate compliance and honor opt-outs. Messages copied into a personal texting app are outside CareText's delivery system.

Sharing and privacy

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers as necessary to operate CareText or comply with law.

Billing and security

Polar handles payment processing. CareText should retain payment references, not complete card or banking credentials. We use access controls, encryption in transit, and other security measures, but cannot guarantee absolute security.
