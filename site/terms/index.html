These terms govern CareText, operated by CyBase Risk Management, LLC, at caretext.app. CareText is currently in private testing. Paid checkout, live SMS, and Trusted Autopilot remain disabled; the paid-plan and SMS terms below describe the program when available.

Using CareText

CareText provides reminders, AI-assisted writing suggestions through MyTouch, and recipient-consented SMS delivery through CareTouch. Subscribers must not send harassment, spam, impersonation, or other prohibited messages. AI-generated content may be inaccurate and should be reviewed.

Subscriptions

Free offers reminders and up to five AI-generated texts monthly, without CareText-delivered SMS. Essential costs $4.99 per month and includes one active loved one and up to 15 CareText-delivered texts per billing month. Plus costs $9.99 per month and includes up to five active loved ones and 100 CareText-delivered texts per billing month in total. Daily CareTouch sending is limited to Plus and available allowances. Unused messages do not roll over.

Recipient replacements

Essential and Plus allow one voluntary completed recipient replacement per rolling 30 days across the account. Verified declined invitations, opt-outs, and incorrect numbers may qualify for exceptions, with documented evidence and review to prevent abuse. All new recipients must opt in before SMS delivery.

Renewals, cancellation and refunds

Paid subscriptions renew automatically monthly or annually according to the checkout selection. Customers cannot change renewal dates in the self-service portal. When paid subscriptions are available, cancel future renewals through the billing portal linked from your account before the next renewal. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not itself refund prior charges. Access continues through the paid period unless a refund or account restriction changes it. Checkout must disclose the actual amount and billing interval before purchase. Refund requests are accepted within seven days of an initial monthly purchase or fourteen days of an initial annual purchase, subject to applicable law and the exceptions described here. Request a refund through customer support and identify the account and purchase; do not send payment credentials. Other requests, including verified service failures or inability to find a consenting recipient, may be reviewed individually. Refunds are not automatic when a recipient opts out.

SMS Messaging Terms

By opting in, you agree to receive recurring automated SMS from CyBase Risk Management, LLC operating as CareText, on behalf of the sender named in your invitation. Messages may be AI-assisted and, for eligible senders, automatically generated through Trusted Autopilot. Frequency varies by sender schedule, up to one planned outgoing CareText per day per recipient relationship, subject to plan limits. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Consent is not a condition of purchase. For support contact support@caretext.app.
