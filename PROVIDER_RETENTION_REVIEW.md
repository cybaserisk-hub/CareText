## Operational implementation required

- Track verified request identity, eligibility, deadlines, legal holds, completion and reasons for retaining records without retaining excessive verification documents.
- Implement account/profile/message deletion or anonymization within the approved target; account for references, audit constraints, files, provider records and retries.
- Define consent retention trigger precisely: withdrawal or last relevant communication may occur at different times. Owner/legal review must resolve the intended clock before code is written.
- Preserve minimal opt-out suppression separately from removable content; prohibit new sends after revocation.
- Implement deletion records outside restored snapshots; quarantine restores and replay deletions/opt-outs before service resumes. Test this before claiming deleted data cannot reappear.
- Inventory all backups, exports, log drains and staff downloads. Apply lifecycle rules and document provider exceptions; do not infer them from dashboard visibility.
- Confirm who owns privacy requests, five-business-day acknowledgment and 30-calendar-day response tracking, extensions and appeals. Verify monitored inbound delivery first.
- Legal review: jurisdiction-specific rights, appeals and deadlines; consent/accounting evidence retention; legal holds; the refund exceptions and program disclosures. No proposed numeric period is characterized as a universal Texas statutory requirement.

**Result:** partial documented capability verification only. Production retention commitments remain blocked on configuration evidence, implementation and restore/deletion tests.
